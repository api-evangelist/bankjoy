# Bankjoy

> Bankjoy is an end-to-end digital banking platform purpose-built for community banks and credit unions, delivering consumer and business online/mobile banking, online account opening, online loan origination, statements, and fraud tooling to 85+ financial institutions and 1M+ members. It is a B2B SaaS provider and does not publish a public developer API; integrations are delivered through banking cores (Corelation KeyStone, Fiserv) and 150+ partner integrations.

## Products
- [Consumer Banking](https://bankjoy.com/products/consumer-banking): Online and mobile banking for retail members.
- [Business Banking](https://bankjoy.com/products/business-banking): Digital banking for business account holders.
- [Online Account Opening](https://bankjoy.com/products/online-account-opening): Digital account opening.
- [Online Loan Application](https://bankjoy.com/products/online-loan-application): Digital loan origination.
- [JoyCompass](https://bankjoy.com/products/joycompass): Growth and engagement tooling.
- [FraudSense](https://bankjoy.com/products/fraudsense): Fraud detection add-on.
- [Statements](https://bankjoy.com/products/statements): Digital statements and documents.
